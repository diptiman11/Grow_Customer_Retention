# Improving User Retention on Groww

## Project Overview

**Improving User Retention on Groww** is a product management case study focused on understanding why users explore Groww but fail to remain active investors, and proposing product features to improve long-term engagement and retention.

The project combines **primary user research, competitor analysis, user personas, customer journey mapping, pain-point analysis, feature prioritization, product design, success metrics, and go-to-market planning**.

> **Project type:** Product Management / Product Strategy Case Study  
> **Focus:** User Retention, Engagement, Financial Product UX  
> **Platform:** Groww

---

## Problem Statement

The project identifies a significant retention challenge: many users download and explore an investment platform but do not continue investing.

The research highlighted four major contributors:

1. **Lack of investment knowledge and confidence** — more than half of surveyed users were not confident about their investing knowledge.
2. **Short-term investment mindset** — a significant portion of users were primarily focused on short-term gains.
3. **Poor customer support experience** — the research reported an average customer-support rating of **4.4/10**.
4. **Limited initial capital / hesitation to invest** — users may be reluctant to commit their own money when they are inexperienced.

The customer journey also showed friction during **KYC, onboarding, investment discovery, decision-making, and post-purchase/portfolio stages**.

## Research & Discovery

### Primary Research

The project used survey-based primary research to understand:

- User confidence in investing
- Investment goals
- Customer-support experience
- Demand for educational resources
- Willingness to invest through stock baskets
- Reasons for not investing actively

Some key findings used to shape the product strategy:

| Finding | Insight |
|---|---|
| 93% of surveyed users felt educational resources were needed | Strong demand for an in-app learning experience |
| 89.6% indicated they would be more likely to invest in a stock basket | Potential for guided and diversified investing |
| 57% identified lack of money as a reason for not actively investing | Opportunity to reduce the initial financial barrier |
| 50.6% reported limited confidence in investing knowledge/research | Need for education and guided decision-making |
| Customer support received an average score of 4.4/10 | Support experience is a potential retention bottleneck |

## Customer Journey

The project mapped the customer journey across five stages:

**Awareness → Acquisition → Engagement → Purchase → Post-Purchase**

Key friction points identified included:

- Doubts about platform credibility
- Lengthy or confusing KYC
- Information overload
- Difficulty understanding investment options
- Emotional reactions to market losses
- Lack of educational content around volatility and long-term investing
- Perceived brokerage and hidden charges

# Proposed Product Solutions

Three major product features were proposed.

## 1. Groww Learning Hub

A dedicated in-app learning section providing structured financial education and market information.

### Core capabilities

- Beginner-to-advanced investment courses
- Articles and educational videos
- Real-time market news
- Search and filtering
- Trending and frequently searched topics
- Personalized news recommendations
- In-app video player
- Discussion forum for user questions

### Product rationale

The Learning Hub aims to reduce uncertainty by helping users understand financial markets and make more informed decisions.

It can also increase engagement by giving users a reason to return to Groww even when they are not actively making transactions.

### Key risks

- Content overload
- Need for effective filtering and navigation
- Financial-regulatory compliance
- Requirement for strong content quality control

---

## 2. Stock Baskets

Curated collections of stocks grouped by:

- Sector
- Market capitalization
- Investment theme
- Risk level

The objective is to simplify stock selection and encourage diversified investing.

### Core capabilities

- Curated stock collections
- Risk-based categorization
- Sector and market-cap filtering
- Minimum investment information
- CAGR information
- Ability to save baskets
- Guided diversification

### Product rationale

Stock Baskets address the problem of users being overwhelmed by a large number of individual stocks.

They provide a more guided investment experience, particularly for users who do not have the expertise or time to perform extensive stock-level research.

### Key risks

- Users may chase baskets based on historical returns
- Risk and long-term sustainability may be overlooked
- Basket composition requires appropriate financial oversight

---

## 3. Groww Tokens

A proposed mechanism to reduce the initial financial barrier for new investors.

Users receive a coupon, such as ₹500 or ₹1,000, which can be converted into **Groww Invest Money**.

The proposed mechanism makes the token amount usable for investment but non-withdrawable, while profits generated from the investment can be withdrawn.

### Example flow

```text
External coupon
      ↓
User onboards Groww
      ↓
Coupon converted to Groww Invest Money
      ↓
User invests
      ↓
Profit / loss generated
      ↓
Investment capital remains non-withdrawable
      ↓
Eligible profit can be withdrawn
      ↓
User continues investing
```

The project proposes partnerships with external platforms such as **Google Pay, Flipkart, Amazon, and Microsoft Edge** for coupon distribution.

### Product rationale

Groww Tokens are intended to work as a low-risk introduction to investing, allowing first-time users to experience the platform without immediately committing their own capital.

### Key risks

- Regulatory considerations
- Users may prioritize tokens over proper investment research
- Excessive gamification could encourage unhealthy investment behavior

# Feature Prioritization

The project uses a **Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort** style prioritization framework.

The proposed priorities are:

| Feature | Priority rationale |
|---|---|
| Groww Learning Hub | High potential reach and strong confidence in user need |
| Stock Baskets | Strong user interest and meaningful impact on investment discovery |
| Groww Tokens | Addresses the capital barrier but has higher implementation/regulatory complexity |

The earlier pain-point mapping also classified Learning Hub, Stock Baskets, and Groww Tokens as **Must Have** solutions, while personalized price notifications and onboarding improvements were given lower priority.

# Success Metrics

The primary outcome metric is:

### North Star / Primary Metric

**Increase in 3-month cohort user retention rate**

### Groww Learning Hub

- % of users completing the first learning module
- Educational-content views
- % of users returning to educational content
- Average weekly time spent on Groww Learn
- Video like-to-view ratio

### Stock Baskets

- % of users making their first investment through a basket
- Number of users viewing baskets for the first time
- % of users investing after viewing a basket
- Repeat basket-investment rate
- Number of users investing in more than two baskets

### Groww Tokens

- Number of new users using Groww Tokens
- % of tokens used before expiry
- % of users withdrawing token-generated profits
- % of users investing their own money after token expiry

# Go-To-Market & Rollout Strategy

The proposed roadmap follows a phased approach.

## Months 1–3: Preparation & Pre-launch

- Conduct market research
- Study competitors
- Finalize product features
- Develop and test Learning Hub, Stock Baskets, and Groww Tokens
- Create educational content
- Prepare launch messaging
- Begin teaser campaigns

## Month 4: Launch

- Coordinated launch communication
- In-app announcements
- Push notifications
- Social media campaigns
- Email communication
- Webinars / virtual launch events
- Referral incentives
- Limited-time promotions

## Months 5–6: Post-launch & Continuous Engagement

- Webinars and tutorials
- User guides and FAQs
- Monitor feature adoption
- Collect user feedback
- Analyze retention
- Iterate on product features

### Groww Tokens — Phased Rollout

**Phase 1: Soft Launch**

- Launch to approximately 1–2% of users
- Conduct internal testing
- Collect surveys and interviews
- Monitor feature usage and KPIs

**Phase 2: Expanded Rollout**

- Expand to approximately 5–10% of users
- Improve the feature using early feedback
- Continue monitoring adoption and retention

# Expected Product Impact

The proposed strategy aims to create a retention loop:

```text
Learn
  ↓
Understand investing
  ↓
Gain confidence
  ↓
Make a guided investment
  ↓
Track portfolio
  ↓
Return to Groww
  ↓
Learn more / invest again
```

Instead of relying only on transactional activity, Groww can create additional reasons for users to return through **education, guided investing, personalized discovery, and lower barriers to first investment**.

---

# Key Takeaways

- Retention is not only an acquisition problem; it is strongly influenced by the user's ability to confidently take the next investment action.
- Education can reduce uncertainty and create recurring engagement.
- Stock Baskets can simplify investment discovery and encourage diversification.
- Groww Tokens can reduce the psychological and financial barrier to a user's first investment.
- Product success should ultimately be measured through **cohort retention**, supported by feature-level adoption and engagement metrics.
- A phased rollout with controlled experimentation and feedback collection is preferable before broad deployment.
